Our Story

The journey of building HypeAura together.

Our Journey

Working on this website as a team of four Mahmood, Thamer, Ali, and Yousef was a complete experience that taught us a lot about design, coding, time management, and how to work together effectively. Even though each one of us had different strengths, we were able to use them in a way that balanced the workload and helped the project come together smoothly. From the beginning, we agreed that everyone would choose the parts they were naturally good at, and that made the whole process feel more organized and enjoyable.

Team Roles

Thamer role focused heavily on the graphical and content-related side of the website. He was responsible for searching, selecting, and preparing the images that appear throughout our pages. He took this task seriously and treated it as if the website were a real online store. He searched for the highest-quality pictures from Pinterest and other websites, always choosing the ones that best represented the clothing items we wanted to include. After finding the right images, he would extract the links and send everything to Ali for implementation. What motivated him was the belief that visuals play a big role in attracting customers. Thamer chose this task because he genuinely enjoys graphical design and sees it as an essential part of any modern website.

Mahmood played a crucial role during both the early design phase and the technical implementation. He created the Figma mock-ups, which acted as the blueprint for everything we built later. He designed the structure of all six pages and showed us clearly where each section, image, and button should go. Throughout the project, Mahmood asked many questions to Dr. Abdelhameed Fawzy to make sure everything was correct and met the project requirements exactly. On top of the design work, Mahmood contributed significantly to the JavaScript functionality. He worked on the form validation code, making sure the "Register Your Interest" form checked that First and Last Names had at least three characters, the mobile number was exactly eight digits, and the email was properly formatted. He also implemented the functionality to store user data using both localStorage and sessionStorage to permanently save the user's information and sessionStorage to control when the welcome message appears. This allowed the user's first name to display across all pages as a personalized welcome message in the navigation bar. After Ali built the initial website structure, Mahmood took on the responsibility of refining and polishing the design to make it smooth, clean, and user-friendly. This required a lot of patience and attention to detail. He carefully adjusted button sizes, improved spacing, enhanced text readability by adding shadows to hero sections, and ensured consistent styling across all pages. His work transformed the website from functional to visually appealing and easy to use. Additionally, Mahmood helped with the Making Of page by explaining the design reasons behind our choices. Without Mahmood's planning, questioning, refinement work, and technical contributions, the project would have been much harder.

Ali worked on building the initial technical foundation of the website. He took the HTML structure and visual plans and turned them into a working website. Ali wrote the base CSS code, implemented Bootstrap throughout the site, and created the fundamental layout and page structure. His ability to quickly build out all six pages gave us a solid foundation to work with. Ali's technical skills were essential in getting the website up and running. He was patient and collaborative when Mahmood made refinements to improve the user experience. Ali's foundational work was critical to the project's success.

Yousef focused on additional JavaScript functionality and testing of the website. He added helpful error messages and a confirmation message when the form was submitted successfully. Yousef tested the website to make sure all buttons, links, and Bootstrap components worked properly across different pages. His contributions helped ensure the website was interactive and user-friendly instead of just static pages.

Challenges We Faced

Throughout the entire project, we communicated constantly and helped each other whenever something became confusing or challenging. There were moments when the CSS conflicted with the HTML, or the JavaScript needed adjustments, or a layout didn't match the mock-ups. One of the biggest challenges we faced was making sure the form validation worked correctly and that the user's name appeared on all pages after registration using both localStorage and sessionStorage. Another challenge was refining the visual design to make the text readable on hero images without using visible boxes, which required experimenting with text shadows and overlay opacity. We also had to ensure our mock-ups were proper wireframes from Figma and not screenshots of the completed website, as that would result in zero marks. Every time we encountered an issue, we discussed it as a group and found a solution together. This teamwork made the final result stronger than if each of us had worked alone.

What We Learned

Overall, this project taught us how to plan, design, build, and finish a complete website from scratch while balancing our different roles. We learned the importance of asking questions early to avoid mistakes later. We discovered how Bootstrap makes styling faster and more consistent across pages. We understood how JavaScript brings interactivity to a website and how localStorage and sessionStorage can work together to save user information and control display logic. We also learned the value of patience and attention to detail when refining a design to make it truly user-friendly. We learned how critical it is to follow project requirements exactly, especially details like having exactly six pages, proper form validation, and including proper wireframes instead of screenshots. Most importantly, we learned that communication and teamwork are essential when working on a group project. Each of us contributed something unique, and all our pieces came together to create a website we are proud of and confident will meet all the grading criteria.
